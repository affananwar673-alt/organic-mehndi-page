Before you apply

Follow the instructions on your actual product label. These general reminders do not replace product-specific directions.

STEP 01

Patch test

Test a small area as directed by the product label before wider use. Do not use if irritation occurs.

STEP 02

Check the label

Read all ingredients and directions. Avoid use if you know you are sensitive to any listed ingredient.

STEP 03

Store safely

Use the storage conditions and shelf life specified for your product and batch.
